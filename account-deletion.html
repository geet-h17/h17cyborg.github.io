lock inn does not host a cloud account of its own. Optional Google Sign-In is only used to sync calendar. Agenda data lives on your Android device. Follow the steps below to delete the in-app Google session and all associated local data.

How to delete your lock inn account

  1. Open the lock inn app on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the gear icon (Settings) in the top-right.
  3. If you signed in with Google: tap Sign out under Account. This ends the Google session in lock inn and removes synced Google Calendar events from the app.
  4. Uninstall lock inn, or go to Android Settings → Apps → lock inn → Storage → Clear data, then uninstall.
  5. If you already uninstalled the app, email h17devs@gmail.com with the subject “lock inn account deletion” and the Google email you used (if any). We will confirm when the request is complete.

What is deleted

  • Tasks, reminders, lists, notes, and lock-screen settings stored in the app
  • Copied lock-wallpaper photos stored in the app’s private storage
  • Google Sign-In tokens stored on the device
  • Google Calendar events that were synced into lock inn (they are removed from the app on sign-out / clear data)

What is kept

  • Your Google account itself — we cannot delete that. Manage it at Google Account settings.
  • Events that live in Google Calendar or another calendar app on the phone — those stay unless you delete them there.
  • Google Play purchase records for lock inn Pro — retained by Google Play under Play’s policies. Uninstalling does not refund or erase the Play entitlement; you can restore Pro later on the same Play account.
  • An email you send us to request deletion — kept only until we finish the request (see retention below), then deleted.

Retention

We do not operate a server that stores your agenda. Once you sign out and clear data or uninstall, local lock inn data is gone immediately. Email deletion requests are handled within 30 days, then we delete that correspondence. Play and Google retain their own records for as long as their policies require.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can remove data and stay signed in, or disconnect Google without uninstalling lock inn.

  1. Open lock inn.
  2. Delete individual tasks, reminders, or lists in the app.
  3. To disconnect Google Calendar only: Settings → Account → Sign out. Local tasks and reminders stay on the device; synced Google events are removed from lock inn.
  4. To remove a lock-wallpaper photo: Lock Studio → change or remove the photo background.
  5. Or email h17devs@gmail.com with the subject “lock inn data deletion” and what you want removed.
