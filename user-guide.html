Start Navigation Screens Lock Pro FAQ

Start

  1. Finish the intro.
  2. On Today, tap Show on lock screen — or Not now if you want to look around first.
  3. Open Tasks, add a task. It should show up on Today.
  4. Tap the lock-screen button on the right of the dock. When status says Live, lock the phone and check the wallpaper.

You do not need Google Calendar to use the app. Sign in later if you want events from Google.

Navigation

The dock sits at the bottom of the screen, in two groups.

  • Left: four tabs — Today, Calendar, Tasks, and Reminders. Tap an icon to open that screen. The one you are on is highlighted.
  • Right: a separate Lock screen button. That opens the lock wallpaper editor.

Settings is the gear in the top-right corner of Today, Calendar, Tasks, and Reminders.

Add things on the screen you are looking at: type a task on Tasks, tap + on an hour in Calendar, or type in Remind me… on Reminders.

Screens

Today

The date is at the top, with how many events, tasks, and reminders you have today. If there is nothing, it says Nothing today. Three tiles underneath open Tasks, Calendar, or Reminders.

Events show one time window, not two clocks. Tap a task or event to open it. Tap a reminder to go to reminders.

Calendar

Switch Day, Week, Month, or Agenda. On Week and Day, tap + on an hour to add an event. That event stays on this phone. It is never written to Google.

Google Calendar lives in Settings → account. Sign-in is read-only and only pulls about yesterday through the next two weeks.

  • Sign in with Google brings events in.
  • Sync now pulls again.
  • Sign out removes those Google events from the phone. Tasks and events you created here stay.

Allow calendar reads events already stored on the device, still without writing back.

Tasks

Color dots choose a list. You only see that list. Create one with + (name, color, optional icon) if none exists yet.

Type in the field and submit to add. Typing also filters. Check a card to complete it; tap the card to open it. There is no full edit screen yet — due is a date, not a time.

Reminders

Remind me… creates an enabled reminder one hour from now. Each row has an Enabled switch; tap the row to change time or delete. The alert says Due now. There is no snooze.

Opening an item

Task

Complete or Delete.

Event

Open in Calendar, Edit event, or Delete event.

A change to a Google-synced event is local. The next Sync now puts Google’s copy back. Reminders do not open this screen.

Lock screen

Tap Lock screen on the right of the dock. That page is where you turn the lock wallpaper on and choose how it looks. lock inn writes a still image to the lock wallpaper only. It never covers PIN, pattern, or password, and it never changes your home wallpaper.

  • Show on lock screen turns it on.
  • Update now rewrites the image — use this if another app took the lock wallpaper.
  • Remove restores the previous lock image.

Status should read Live. If the phone says Wallpaper changes are blocked, allow it in Settings. A few devices cannot set a lock wallpaper at all.

Under more options: how many rows (4 free, 8 Pro), which kinds to show, and Hide titles — names become Busy, Task, or Reminder. Times stay.

Settings

Gear, top right. Most rows are a label and one action. Two cards at lock screen need attention only when something is wrong: wallpaper blocked, or battery pausing background updates.

Turn on Reminder alerts. If Exact reminders appears, allow it so alerts can fire on time. Theme follows the phone.

FAQ

Google says Access blocked, or shows LockDesk

Sign-in is limited until Google finishes verifying the app. The sheet may still say LockDesk — that is lock inn, not another product. Email h17devs@gmail.com with the Gmail you used.

I signed in. Why isn’t Pro unlocked?

Calendar sign-in and Pro are different. Sign in with Google only syncs calendar. Get pro follows the Play account on the phone. Signing out of calendar does not remove Pro.

A Google event came back — or a new event isn’t on Google

lock inn does not write to Google Calendar. Delete or edit a synced event in Google Calendar, then sync. Add event in lock inn stays on this phone.

The lock wallpaper didn’t change

Lock the phone — it never appears on home. Then:

  1. Open Lock screen on the dock. Status should say Live. If it says Off, tap Show on lock screen.
  2. If the preview is right but the phone is not, tap Update now.
  3. If wallpaper is blocked, tap Allow in Settings, then update.
The PIN still shows

That is correct. Android’s unlock screen always sits on top. If the PIN pad is missing, email us — do not send a photo of the code.

Reminders are late or silent

Turn on Reminder alerts. Allow Exact reminders if you see that row. If Battery says updates may pause, tap Review and exempt lock inn. The reminder’s Enabled switch must be on.

The lock image is empty

It only draws what you enabled, up to the row cap. Check what to show, which list is selected, and Hide titles. If today says Nothing today, lock is empty on purpose. Google events outside yesterday–two weeks ahead will not appear.

Restore Pro, sign out, or delete data

Restore on the same Play account via Restore purchases. Sign out drops synced Google events; everything you created here stays. To wipe the phone copy: sign out, then clear app data or uninstall. Play keeps Pro. Steps: Delete account and data.

Offline, widgets, light and dark

Tasks, reminders, local events, and a lock image already applied work offline. Sign-in, sync, and purchases need a network. There is no home-screen widget. Theme follows the phone.
