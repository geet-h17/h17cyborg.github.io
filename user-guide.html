lock inn — how to use it

Last updated October 30, 2026 · Version 1.7.1 · Android 8 and up

Home How to use Privacy Terms Delete account

lock inn is a local-first desk for Android. Tasks, calendar, reminders — then today’s agenda painted onto the lock wallpaper. Home wallpaper stays home. The system lock screen sits on top of that image, so PIN, pattern, and password entry stay exactly where Android put them.

This guide matches version 1.7.1. If a control is not named here, it is not in the build you have.

The idea

Your actual day, parked by the clock.

lock inn writes a generated lock wallpaper through Android’s wallpaper API, lock flag only. It is a still image that refreshes when your day changes. It is not a live overlay, not draw-over-apps, not a floating widget, and not a home-screen widget. Unlock the phone the way you always did.

Desk features — today, calendar, tasks, reminders — are free. Pro only buys wallpaper cosmetics: extra cards, a photo background, and up to eight rows.

First two minutes

Open the app. The intro is short. After it, today may show put it on lock. One tap on Show on lock screen writes today’s desk onto the lock wallpaper. Continue if you want it now; Not now if you want to look around first. Calendar sign-in and alerts wait in settings — they are optional.

  1. Add a task on tasks. You should see it on today (undated open tasks count as today).
  2. Open the right-hand pill. Confirm status is Live.
  3. Lock the phone. The agenda should sit under the system clock. The PIN pad is still the PIN pad.

Google Calendar is optional. Local tasks, local events, and reminders already fill the desk.

Finding your way around

Two floating pills at the bottom. Not one bar.

  • Left pill — four tabs, icons only: today, calendar, tasks, reminders. The active tab fills a circle in that section’s color (amber, teal, mauve, slate) and slides across.
  • Right pill — lock screen. Brand chrome, not a fifth tab. This is Lock Studio, titled lock screen.
  • Gear, top right on every main screen — settings.

Create lives on each section: the tasks field, the calendar hour +, the reminders field. There is no extra create button between the pills.

Today

Control center. The date is the hero — large, bold. Under it: Hi, or Hi, plus your first name if Google Sign-In gave the app one.

Counts sit under that: events, tasks, reminders due today, in the section’s ink. A quiet day says Nothing today.

Three glance tiles underneath:

  • Tasks — remaining today. Tap jumps to tasks.
  • Next event — next timed event. Tap jumps to calendar.
  • Next reminder — next fire time. Tap jumps to reminders.

Empty tiles read None.

Below that, today’s cards. Neutral surfaces, a color edge bar, a bold title. Timed events show one window (6:00 – 8:00 PM) plus a muted duration pill (45 min, 2 h). Not two separate clocks. Instant items (a task due today, a reminder) keep the time on the title row. All-day events say All day.

The next or current item wears a faint tint. Past timed events drop into Earlier. An empty list says Nothing on the desk today.

Tap a task or event to open it. Tap a reminder card to jump to reminders — reminders have no detail screen.

Calendar

Four views in the top bar: Day, Week, Month, Agenda.

  • Month — date grid. Color dots under days that have items. Tap a date to open it.
  • Week — each day is a full-width band. Large date and weekday on the left; today’s numeral in calendar teal. Hour marks on the right, with a circular + and event chips.
  • Day — the same band language, one day at a time.
  • Agenda — a chronological scroll of those day bands, then that day’s items.

Add event is local. Title, Date, All day, Start, End, optional Notes and Location. Save writes to this phone. It does not write to Google Calendar. Events you create here will not appear in the Google Calendar app.

Empty range: Nothing here.

Google Calendar (read-only)

Settings → account. Row title Google Calendar. Body: Brings in events from Google.

  • Sign in with Google — pulls events. Window is the past 1 day and the next 14. Older and further-out events stay on Google; they are not in lock inn.
  • Sync now — pull again. Needs a network.
  • Sign out — Removes Google calendar events from this phone. Your tasks stay. Hand-created local events stay too.

This is read-only. Deletes you make in lock inn on a synced event are local. The next Sync now puts that event back. Edits to a synced event work the same way — the next pull replaces Google-sourced rows.

Allow calendar is the fallback. Body: Reads events on this phone so they can reach the lock card. That is the device calendar permission, not Google Sign-In. Use it when you want events already on the phone without signing in. Also read-only.

If Google Sign-In shows Access blocked and mentions LockDesk, that is still lock inn — Google has not finished verifying the project yet. Email h17devs@gmail.com with the Gmail you used and we will add you. Retrying the same account will not fix it.

Tasks

A row of color dots at the top is the list selector (Lists). The selected list’s name is the page title, in that list’s color. If you have not created a list yet, the title falls back to Inbox — create a list with the + on the rail first. You only see that list’s tasks. Never all lists at once.

The field reads Add a task to the selected list. Submit creates into that list with silent defaults: no date, no time, no priority. Typing in the field filters the visible list live. No matches: None match. Empty list: Type above to add one. All done: Nothing open in this list.

Tap the check on a card to complete it. Tap the card itself to open the item. Undated open tasks still show on today and can appear on the lock image.

There is no Edit task button in this build. Due is a date, not a time — you will not find a due-time picker. Quick-add and complete are the task flow.

New list

Tap the + on the list rail. Sheet title New list. Name, eight color swatches, optional icon (Home, Work, Book, Heart, Cart, Flag), one Create.

Reminders

Field: Remind me… Submit creates an enabled reminder one hour from now. That default is silent — the field does not spell it out.

Each row has an Enabled switch. Off means it will not fire. Tap the card to open Edit reminder: Title, Date, Start, optional Notes, Enabled, Delete reminder. There is no reminder-offset field and no snooze.

When it fires, the notification says Due now. That is the alert. There is no snooze action on it.

Groups: upcoming first, then Earlier. Empty: None yet. Nothing upcoming: Nothing coming up.

Opening an item

Tap a task or event on today, tasks, or calendar. That is the detail screen. Reminders stay on their own list.

Header is flat — title, date and time. No filled card.

Tiles underneath, when they apply: a countdown (Starts in), List, Where. Notes sit below as a quiet list.

Bottom pills:

  • Task: Complete (filled) and Delete.
  • Event: Open in Calendar (filled — opens the system calendar, not a write-back), Edit event, Delete event. A synced event with a ticket link may also show Open ticket.

Edit event is the same local sheet as Add event. For a Google-synced event, the next Sync now will replace your local edit. Delete event on a synced row behaves the same way — it comes back on the next pull. Delete it in Google Calendar if you want it gone for good.

No snooze on this screen. Reminders do not open here.

Lock screen

Right pill. Page title: lock screen. Large phone preview on a quiet stage.

Status line:

  • Off — not writing a lock wallpaper.
  • Ready to apply — enabled, not yet on the device.
  • Live · updated {time} — the image is on the lock wallpaper.

Actions:

  • Show on lock screen — enable and write.
  • Update now — rewrite the current image. Use this if another app took the lock wallpaper, or if the preview looks right and the device does not.
  • Remove — restore the previous lock wallpaper. Home is not touched.

Caption under the looks: Home wallpaper stays. If another app replaces the lock image: If another app replaces it, tap Update now.

The image sits behind the system clock and keyguard. Your PIN, pattern, or password is never covered. That is architecture, not a setting.

OEM and Android builds vary. Some phones report This phone can’t set a lock screen. or Wallpaper changes are blocked. Settings will show that on the Wallpaper card. We cannot override a manufacturer that blocks the API.

Collapsed under more options (body: Layout, what appears, and privacy.): Show / hide options.

  • layout — Text size (Small, Medium, Large). Vertical position slider; Higher stays clear of the clock. Up to N items (free max 4, Pro max 8). Alignment (Start, Center).
  • what to show — Events, Tasks, Reminders. With more than one task list, a List chip row (All plus each list name). Show tomorrow after today ends.
  • privacy — Hide titles (body: Shows Busy or Task instead. Events become Busy, tasks Task, reminders Reminder). Updated time stamps the image.

Filters are why a full desk can still look empty on lock. Check these before assuming a bug.

Permissions, briefly

Asked when a feature needs them. None of these is an overlay permission. lock inn does not request draw-over-apps.

  • Calendar read — Allow calendar, so on-device events can reach the desk and the lock image.
  • Notifications — Reminder alerts, and the reminder channel (Due now.).
  • Exact alarm — Exact reminders, so the alert can fire on the minute.
  • Set wallpaper — lock wallpaper only.
  • Boot — so reminders and the lock image can recover after reboot.
  • Internet — Google Calendar pull, Play purchase and restore.
  • Billing — lockinn_pro.

Refuse any of these and the matching feature stays off. The rest of the desk keeps working.

What stays on the phone

Tasks, lists, reminders, hand-created events, lock-screen settings, and a chosen photo copy live in the app’s on-device store. No lock inn cloud account.

Needs a network: Sign in with Google, Sync now, Get pro, Restore.

Works offline: the desk, local events, reminders already scheduled, and the lock wallpaper already applied. The image will not pick up a new Google event until you sync again.

Sign-out, clear data, and uninstall are covered in the FAQ and on Delete account and data.

FAQ

Google says Access blocked, or mentions LockDesk

Google has not finished verifying lock inn yet, so Sign-In is limited to accounts we have approved. The sheet may still say LockDesk — that is the same project, not a different app.

Email h17devs@gmail.com with the Gmail you tapped. Once we add it, Sign-In will work. Trying again with the same account before that will keep failing.

I signed in with Google. Why isn’t Pro unlocked?

Calendar and Pro use two different Google accounts, and they do not unlock each other.

  • Sign in with Google in Settings → account is only for Calendar.
  • Get pro and Restore purchases follow the Google Play account on the phone.

You can sync calendar on one Gmail and own Pro on another. Signing out of Calendar never removes Pro.

I deleted a Google event in lock inn. It came back.

lock inn reads Google Calendar. It does not own those events. The next Sync now copies Google’s version back onto the phone, so a local delete does not last.

To remove it for good, delete it in Google Calendar, then sync. Events you created with Add event in lock inn are local — those stay gone when you delete them here.

I created an event here. Why isn’t it on Google Calendar?

Add event saves on this phone only. lock inn never writes to Google Calendar. If you need the event on Google as well, create it in the Google Calendar app, then sync. Open in Calendar on a synced event opens Google’s copy; it does not upload a local one.

The lock screen didn’t change

lock inn paints the lock wallpaper, not the home screen. Lock the phone to see it. Then:

  1. Open the right pill. Status should read Live. If it says Off, tap Show on lock screen.
  2. If the in-app preview looks right but the phone does not, tap Update now.
  3. If another wallpaper app took the lock image, tap Update now again. You may need to stop that other app, or it will keep replacing ours.
  4. In Settings, if the Wallpaper card says Wallpaper changes are blocked., tap Allow, then update.
  5. If the phone says This phone can’t set a lock screen., that device cannot host this feature.
The PIN, pattern, or password still shows. Is the lock image broken?

No. Android’s unlock screen always sits on top of the wallpaper. lock inn never covers PIN, pattern, or password entry — that is how the feature is built.

If you cannot see the unlock pad, that would be the bug. Email us right away, without a photo of the PIN or pattern.

Did lock inn change my home wallpaper?

It should not. Only the lock wallpaper is written. If home changed, another app or the phone’s wallpaper picker likely applied both.

Remove restores the previous lock wallpaper. It cannot repair a home image something else replaced. If home moved after using lock inn, email us the phone model so we can check that device.

Reminders are late, or they don’t fire

Work through Settings, then the reminder itself:

  • Reminder alerts must be on. If it says Reminders won’t alert you., tap Turn on.
  • If Exact reminders is visible, tap Allow. Without it, Android may delay the alarm.
  • If the Battery card says Background updates may pause., tap Review and exempt lock inn. Some phones still need a battery exception in system settings.
  • The reminder’s Enabled switch must be on.

There is no snooze. Once a reminder has fired, it will not alert again.

Nothing shows on the lock image

Today can be full and the lock image still quiet. The lock screen only draws what you asked it to, up to the row limit.

  • Under more options → what to show, turn on Events, Tasks, or Reminders as needed.
  • List may be pinned to one task list instead of All.
  • Up to N items may be low, so earlier items filled the slots.
  • Show tomorrow after today ends may be off after the day is over.
  • Hide titles may be on — the rows are there, labeled Busy, Task, or Reminder.
  • Google only syncs about the past day and the next two weeks, so a later event will not appear yet.
  • If today itself says Nothing today, the lock image is correctly empty.
What does Hide titles do?

On the lock wallpaper only, Hide titles replaces names with Busy, Task, or Reminder. Times stay. Turn it off to show real titles again. Screens inside the app never hide titles.

I got a new phone. How do I get Pro back?

Install lock inn from Play, signed into the same Google Play account that paid. Then Settings → Restore purchases → Restore. You should see Pro restored.

Calendar Sign-In is separate — sign into Calendar again if you want events. No purchase on this Play account. means this Play user did not buy Pro, or you are on a different Play account than the one that paid.

What happens when I Sign out?

Sign out ends Google Calendar in lock inn and removes synced Google events from this phone. You will see Signed out. Synced calendar events were removed from this phone.

Tasks, lists, reminders, events you created in lock inn, lock-screen settings, and Pro all stay. Google Calendar on Google’s side is untouched — we never write to it, so we never delete it there.

How do I uninstall or delete my data?

lock inn does not keep a cloud account of its own. Everything on the desk lives on the phone.

  1. Sign out if you signed in.
  2. Android Settings → Apps → lock inn → Storage → clear data, or uninstall. That removes the on-device desk, sign-in tokens, and any copied lock photo.

Play keeps the Pro purchase on the Play account. Uninstalling does not refund it; Restore brings it back on a new install. Google Calendar keeps Google’s copy of your events.

Full steps: Delete account and data.

Does it work offline?

Yes for the desk: local tasks, local events, reminders already scheduled, and a lock image already applied. Sign in with Google, Sync now, Get pro, and Restore need a network. A new Google event will not reach the lock image until you sync again.

Can I pick light or dark in the app?

Not from lock inn. Theme follows the phone and shows System. Change light or dark in Android display settings.

Is there a home-screen widget?

No. The day lives on the lock wallpaper. The home screen is left alone on purpose.

What should I expect in this beta?

This is an early build. The desk and lock wallpaper are the product. A few things are still simple on purpose:

  • Tasks are quick-add and complete. There is no full edit sheet on screen yet, and due dates are dates, not times.
  • Events you add here stay on the phone. Google Calendar is read-only, about one day back and two weeks ahead.
  • Reminders do not snooze.
  • There is no home-screen widget and no in-app theme picker.
  • On some phones another app can steal the lock wallpaper. Update now puts lock inn back.
  • Google Sign-In may still show the name LockDesk. That is us.

If something fails in a way this page does not describe, tell us. That is the useful kind of report.
