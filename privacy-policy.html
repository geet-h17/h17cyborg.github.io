lock inn — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Home Privacy Terms

lock inn (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) shows your tasks, events, and reminders on your Android lock screen. This policy explains what information the app uses, what stays on your phone, and what third parties may process when you use optional features.

1. Information we use

  • Agenda data (on-device): Tasks, reminders, notes, lists, and lock-screen settings you create are stored locally on your device (app database / preferences).
  • Google account (optional): If you sign in with Google to sync calendar, we receive your name and email through Google Sign-In, and temporary credentials needed to read your Google Calendar. Tokens are stored securely on your device. Signing out removes synced Google calendar events from the app.
  • Device calendar (optional): With permission, the app can read calendar events on your phone so they can appear in lock inn and on the lock wallpaper.
  • Photos (optional, Pro): If you choose a photo as a lock wallpaper background, a copy is stored in the app’s private storage on your device to render the wallpaper. Photos are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Purchases: If you buy lock inn Pro, Google Play processes payment. We receive purchase / entitlement status, not your full payment card details.
  • Notifications & alarms (optional): With permission, the app schedules reminders and can refresh the lock wallpaper after reboot or time changes.
  • Crash reports (if enabled): If Firebase Crashlytics is configured for a build, anonymous crash diagnostics (device model, OS version, stack traces) may be sent to Google Firebase to help us fix bugs. Current beta builds may ship without this enabled.

2. How we use information

  • Show your day in the app and on the lock-screen wallpaper you enable.
  • Sync calendar events you choose to connect.
  • Restore Pro purchases via Google Play.
  • Send reminder notifications you configure.
  • Diagnose crashes when crash reporting is enabled.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not show ads in the app.
  • We do not operate our own cloud backend that stores your agenda for this product today — your tasks and settings stay on your device unless a third-party service you connect (Google Calendar / Play) processes them.
  • We do not change your home-screen wallpaper; lock wallpaper uses Android’s lock wallpaper API.
  • We do not cover or intercept your PIN / pattern / password entry.

4. Third-party services

Depending on features you use, these providers may process data under their own policies:

  • Google Sign-In & Google Calendar — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing — Google Payments
  • Firebase Crashlytics (if enabled) — Firebase Privacy

5. Data retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete items, clear app data, or uninstall. Synced Google Calendar data remains governed by Google. Purchase records are retained by Google Play. Sign-out removes synced Google events from the local app database.

6. Children

lock inn is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help you remove local access by uninstall guidance.

7. Your choices

  • Skip Google sign-in — the app works with local tasks/reminders; calendar sync needs sign-in.
  • Deny calendar, notification, or alarm permissions — related features won’t work.
  • Sign out in Settings to clear the Google session and synced Google events from the app.
  • Uninstall the app to remove local data.
  • Manage Play purchases / subscriptions in Google Play.

8. Security

OAuth tokens are stored using on-device secure storage. We rely on Android platform protections and Google’s services for network security. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

9. International users

If you use Google or Firebase services, data may be processed in countries where those providers operate (including the United States). By using those features, you acknowledge that processing.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
